About the Firm

Consumer Protection

Consumer Disputes & Consumer Commission Proceedings

Consumers may have remedies where goods are defective, services are deficient, or an unfair or restrictive trade practice causes legally recognised harm. We assist in assessing the dispute, preparing the complaint and pursuing appropriate relief under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

Types of consumer disputes

Matters we can assist with

  • Deficiency in services by banks and financial institutions
  • Unauthorised charges, service failures and banking-related consumer disputes
  • Insurance claim delays, repudiation and settlement disputes
  • Defective products, warranty failures and replacement/refund disputes
  • Builder, developer and housing-related service disputes, where maintainable
  • Real-estate services and delayed or incomplete promised services
  • Medical and healthcare service disputes, subject to legal maintainability and facts
  • Telecom, internet, courier, travel and other service-related complaints
  • Misleading advertisements and unfair trade practices
  • Failure to deliver goods or provide services as promised
  • Non-refund of deposits, advance payments or consideration
  • Defective or incomplete event, photography and videography services
How we assist

From initial review to final relief

  1. Initial case assessment: Review invoices, receipts, agreements, messages, emails, warranty papers and other records to identify the consumer relationship, cause of action and appropriate forum.
  2. Pre-litigation steps: Prepare legal notices and replies, organise evidence and consider whether a settlement or other remedy is appropriate.
  3. Complaint drafting: Prepare the consumer complaint, list of dates, parties, facts, grounds, supporting affidavit, document list and specific prayer for relief.
  4. Filing and procedure: Assist with filing before the appropriate District, State or National Consumer Commission, subject to territorial and pecuniary jurisdiction and applicable procedure.
  5. Proceedings: Prepare responses to the opposite party's version, evidence affidavits, written arguments and applications, and represent the client at hearings.
  6. Relief and enforcement: Seek appropriate orders such as removal of defects, replacement, refund, compensation, costs or discontinuance of an unfair practice, as supported by law and evidence; assist with execution where required.
  7. Appeals: Advise on and prepare appeals or other remedies against orders, subject to limitation, deposit requirements and statutory conditions.
Consumer forum jurisdiction, limitation, maintainability and the relief available depend on the transaction value, consideration paid, place of cause of action, nature of service and current statutory rules. No particular result is guaranteed.
